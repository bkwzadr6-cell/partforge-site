Questions Buyers Ask Before Sending Drawings

What is your minimum order quantity?

MOQ is 50 pieces for production runs. There is no minimum for samples and prototypes — one-off parts are welcome.

How fast can I get a quotation and samples?

Quotation with free DFM feedback within 24 hours of receiving your drawing. Samples typically ship in 7 days; urgent jobs can be flagged in the RFQ form.

Which drawing formats do you accept?

STEP, IGES, PDF, DWG and DXF. Attach files up to 8 MB in the form, or paste a cloud link (Drive / Dropbox) for larger STEP or ZIP files.

Can you sign an NDA before I send drawings?

Yes. Write "NDA required" in Project Details and we sign before you upload anything. Drawings are only shared with the engineers quoting your part.

What tolerance and inspection documents do you provide?

Sustained tolerance down to ±0.01 mm. Every shipment includes dimensional inspection reports; full material certificates and CMM data are available on request.

How do shipping and payment work?

Shipping terms (EXW, FOB or DDP, by air or sea) and payment terms are confirmed in each quotation — typical payment is T/T. Tell us your destination country in the form so the first quote already includes a shipping option.

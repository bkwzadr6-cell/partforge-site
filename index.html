FAQ

Questions buyers ask before the first order

What do I have to send to get a quote?

A 3D model (STEP / IGES) or a 2D drawing (PDF / DWG / DXF) with the critical tolerances marked, plus quantity, material and surface finish. If you only have a sample or a photo, send that and describe the part — we will reverse it to a drawing and confirm with you before quoting.

How fast will you answer, and what does the quote include?

Within 24 working hours for a typical single part. You get unit price at your quantities, lead time, the process we plan to use, and a short DFM note if we think a change would save money or risk. Bigger assemblies or many SKUs may need 2–3 days; we say so rather than going quiet.

What is your minimum order quantity?

1–5 pcs for prototypes and samples. 50 pcs is our normal production MOQ, but low-volume repeat work is fine — many buyers start with 20–30 pcs to validate the fit before committing.

What tolerance and surface finish can you actually hold?

±0.01 mm typical on milled and turned parts, ±0.005 mm on precision features we agree in advance, turned diameters to ±0.008 mm. Ra 0.8–1.6 on critical faces is achievable with the right setup. Anything tighter, we discuss whether it is a machining problem or a grinding problem — and price it honestly.

Do you provide inspection reports and material certificates?

Material certificate with every order. CMM dimensional report per batch on request, and first-article inspection before series production so you approve the measurement record, not just a sample part.

Will my drawing stay confidential?

Yes. Files are used for quoting and production only, access limited to the people running your job. Send the NDA before you upload anything and we will sign and return it.

How do payment and shipping work?

Usually 50% deposit and the balance before shipping; PayPal, T/T and Western Union are accepted for sample orders, T/T for production. We arrange DHL / FedEx / UPS courier, air or sea freight, with commercial invoice and packing list, and send tracking the moment the pallet leaves.

What if a batch does not match the drawing?

Tell us within 30 days with photos and the dimensions concerned. We re-measure against your drawing. Confirmed non-conformance means remake or credit, and we cover the freight. The root cause is written into our process so the next batch does not repeat it.

Ready to see what your part costs?

Send one drawing. If we are not the right shop for it, we will tell you in the same 24 hours — with a note on why.
